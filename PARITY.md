# Wii U → Android: everything to bring over

Made from the Wii U game's own code (seanmallan316/kingdomkart). Every line here must be in the Android
version. ✅ done · 🔨 in progress · ⬜ not started. The Android build pulls the Wii U repo's data (roads,
characters, story map, cups, pictures, models, music, sounds, voices) on every build so it never falls behind.

## Front of the game
- 🔨 Opening cutscene (plays again if the title screen is left alone)
- 🔨 Title screen (the title art), main menu — biblical, luxurious look, works with touch AND controller
- ⬜ Options: music volume, sound volume, rumble
- ⬜ Records screen (best times, par times, stats)
- ⬜ Credits screen

## Story Mode (Bible Journeys)
- ⬜ The world map folded on a wooden table, opening out panel by panel, the land rising out of the paper
- ⬜ 16 stops in Bible order, Creation to Glory; glowing pins, padlocks until open, gold flags once cleared
- ⬜ Your kart drives along the painted roads from stop to stop
- ⬜ Clear a stop by finishing top 3 (win at Jericho and against Goliath) → the next one opens, celebration
- ⬜ After a race: back to the map and on to the next stop
- ⬜ Each character's own journey drawn on the map with a verse at each stop; "my travels"
- ⬜ Boats, camels, doves, clouds and fire moving on the map; the map's own sounds and music

## Modes
- ⬜ Grand Prix: Gospel Cup, Genesis to Glory (opens with Road to Heaven), Pharaoh's Cup, Babylonian Cup; points
- ⬜ Trophy ceremony with podium, then the cup winner's Bible story scene (22 stories, 3D sets)
- 🔨 Single Race with road select (all 16 roads)
- 🔨 Time Trial: par times, records, ghosts
- ⬜ The Walls of Jericho: 7 laps, shofars, the great shout, the walls fall
- ⬜ David vs Goliath: five stones from the brook, sling, shield-bearer, spears, two armies, two minutes
- ⬜ Multiplayer 2-4 split screen with Bluetooth controllers (join screen, every mode)

## Racing
- 🔨 22 characters (7 + 15 to unlock) with the Wii U stats; 5 kart bodies (Standard, Chariot, Boat, Ark, Sling) and kart select
- 🔨 The Wii U driving: speeds, drifting, mini/super turbos, rocket start, tricks off jumps, boost arrows, bounce pads
- ⬜ Shortcuts, tunnels with crystals, coins (top speed up to 10), obstacles crossing the road, hidden gems, the whale
- 🔨 Eight blessings exactly as the Wii U (incl. coins, trails, parting zone); item window spinning
- ⬜ Bumping gives the other racer a boost; slow-motion finish; wrong way; mini-map; final lap music; rumble
- ⬜ Character voice lines and talking faces
